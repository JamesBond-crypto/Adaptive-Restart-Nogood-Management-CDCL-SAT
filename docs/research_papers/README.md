### Paper 1: Rational Deployment of CSP Heuristics
- **Authors:** David Tolpin and Solomon Eyal Shimony
- **Affiliation:** Department of Computer Science, Ben-Gurion University of the Negev
- **Publication:** *Proceedings of the 22nd International Joint Conference on Artificial Intelligence (IJCAI 2011)*, Barcelona, Catalonia, Spain, July 16–22, 2011, pp. 680–686. AAAI Press.
- **Topic:** Value of Information (VOI), optimal stopping, and rational meta-reasoning for constraint satisfaction heuristics under resource constraints.
- **Official Access:** [IJCAI Proceedings](https://www.ijcai.org/Proceedings/11/Papers/103.pdf) | [Ben-Gurion University Technical Report](https://www.cs.bgu.ac.il/~tolpin/rational-csp.pdf)

### Paper 2: Best Heuristic Identification for Constraint Satisfaction
- **Authors:** Frédéric Koriche, Christophe Lecoutre, Anastasia Paparrizou, and Hugues Wattez
- **Affiliation:** CRIL, Univ. Artois & CNRS / LIX CNRS, École Polytechnique, Institut Polytechnique de Paris
- **Publication:** *Proceedings of the 31st International Joint Conference on Artificial Intelligence and the 25th European Conference on Artificial Intelligence (IJCAI-ECAI 2022)*, Vienna, Austria, July 23–29, 2022, pp. 1859–1865.
- **Topic:** Multi-Armed Bandit (MAB) exploration strategies (Adaptive Single Tournament, UCB1, Successive Rejects) for online selection of variable-ordering heuristics.
- **Official Access:** [DOI: 10.24963/ijcai.2022/259](https://doi.org/10.24963/ijcai.2022/259) | [IJCAI-22 Proceedings](https://www.ijcai.org/proceedings/2022/0259.pdf)

### Paper 3: Can Q-Learning with Graph Networks Learn a Generalizable Branching Heuristic for a SAT Solver?
- **Authors:** Vitaly Kurin, Saad Godil, Shimon Whiteson, and Bryan Catanzaro
- **Affiliation:** Department of Computer Science, University of Oxford / NVIDIA
- **Publication:** *Advances in Neural Information Processing Systems 33 (NeurIPS 2020)*, pp. 9608–9621, 2020.
- **Topic:** Reinforcement learning (Graph-Q-SAT) using bipartite Graph Neural Networks (GNNs) for SAT variable branching heuristics.
- **Official Access:** [NeurIPS Proceedings](https://proceedings.neurips.cc/paper/2020/file/6c44dc630e9ced8d1cf1e39b4b09b53d-Paper.pdf) | [arXiv:1909.11830](https://arxiv.org/abs/1909.11830)

### Paper 4: Solving Dynamic Constraint Satisfaction Problems by Identifying Stable Features
- **Authors:** Richard J. Wallace, Diarmuid Grimes, and Eugene C. Freuder
- **Affiliation:** Cork Constraint Computation Centre, University College Cork
- **Publication:** *Proceedings of the 21st International Joint Conference on Artificial Intelligence (IJCAI 2009)*, Pasadena, California, USA, July 11–17, 2009, pp. 621–626.
- **Topic:** Identification of stable subproblem features, points of contention, and nogood reuse across dynamic constraint satisfaction problems.
- **Official Access:** [IJCAI 2009 Proceedings Archive](https://ijcai.org/proceedings/2009/109)
